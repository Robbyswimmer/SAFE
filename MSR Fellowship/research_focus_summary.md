# Research Focus Summary
**Robert Moseley | UC Riverside | Machine Learning**

My research develops foundational architectures for multimodal LLMs that move beyond tokenization toward modality-aware representations. Rather than forcing all sensory inputs through text-centric tokenization, I design specialized processing pathways that preserve each modality's inherent structure—temporal dynamics for audio, spatial hierarchies for vision. Employing cognitively-inspired gated fusion, parameter-efficient LoRA adaptation, and safety-preserving training, I achieve strong multimodal performance while guaranteeing zero regression on base capabilities. This work combines theoretical rigor with Microsoft production experience (50M+ inferences), advancing both foundational multimodal AI research and inclusive technology accessible to global populations where knowledge exists in non-textual forms.
