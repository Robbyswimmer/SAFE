## Formatting Requirements

- **Page limit**: 14 pages (including figures/tables, excluding references)
- **Style**: Springer LNCS (ECCV 2026 Author Kit — NEW template, different from ECCV 2024)
- **Review**: Double-blind (no author-identifying info)
- **Platform**: OpenReview
- **Rebuttal**: 1 page, two-column format (ECCV 2026 rebuttal template)

## Author Kit

- Overleaf: clone project into your account
- GitHub: https://github.com/eccv-submission/ECCV2026-Author-Kit (clone/fork)
- Also available as ZIP/TAR

## Critical Policies

- No external links in submission/supplementary/rebuttal
- No media/social media discussion until final decision
- Supplementary: videos, proofs, extra figures/tables, code (encouraged)
- Supplementary must NOT contain improved results or updated PDF
- At least one author must register and present in person
- All authors need completed OpenReview profiles by registration deadline
- Author list finalized at submission deadline (Mar 5) — no changes after
- LLM usage allowed but authors responsible for all content (no fake citations)
